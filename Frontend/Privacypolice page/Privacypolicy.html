Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2025

1. Introduction

At MedInsure Predict, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our medical insurance cost prediction services. By using our platform, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

We handle sensitive health and personal data in compliance with applicable laws, including HIPAA standards for protected health information (PHI).

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, age, gender, BMI, smoking status, region, and children count provided during cost prediction.
  • Health-Related Data: Medical charges history, pre-existing conditions (if voluntarily shared for accurate predictions).
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site (via cookies).
  • Prediction Inputs: Data entered for insurance cost predictions, such as demographic and lifestyle factors.

We do not collect sensitive PHI without explicit consent and encryption.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide personalized insurance cost predictions using machine learning models.
  • Improve our services and algorithms based on anonymized data.
  • Send updates, newsletters, or promotional content (with opt-out option).
  • Ensure compliance with legal requirements and prevent fraud.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information in limited circumstances:

  • With service providers (e.g., cloud hosting for data storage) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • If required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or regulatory request.
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice to users).

Anonymized, aggregated data may be used for research or industry reports.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Access controls and regular security audits.
  • Compliance with HIPAA and GDPR where applicable.

While no system is 100% secure, we strive to protect your data to the fullest extent.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance user experience, analyze site traffic (via Google Analytics), and remember preferences. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Our cookies do not store personal health data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, update, or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing.

Contact us to exercise these rights. We respond within 30 days.

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be posted here with the updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
