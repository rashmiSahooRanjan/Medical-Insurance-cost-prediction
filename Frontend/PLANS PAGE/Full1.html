    What's Not Covered

    • Cosmetic or Plastic Surgery
      Procedures done for aesthetic purposes unless medically necessary.
    • Self-Inflicted Injuries
      Injuries or illnesses resulting from attempted suicide or self-harm.
    • War & Nuclear Risks
      Injuries arising from war, invasion, terrorism, or nuclear radiation.
    • Alcohol & Drug Abuse
      Treatments related to alcoholism, drug addiction, or substance abuse.
    • Dental Treatment
      Dental procedures unless requiring hospitalization due to accident.
    • Infertility Treatment
      IVF, GIFT, ZIFT and other assisted reproduction procedures.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who can buy this plan? +

    Anyone between 18 to 65 years of age can purchase this plan. The policy can cover individuals, spouse, dependent children, and parents. Family floater options are also available.

    What is the waiting period? +

    30 days waiting period for all illnesses except accidents (covered from day 1). Pre-existing diseases are covered after 2 years. Specific diseases like cataract, hernia have 1-year waiting period.

    Can I add family members later? +

    Yes, you can add family members at the time of renewal. Additional premium will be charged based on their age and the coverage will start after completion of waiting periods.

    How do I renew my policy? +

    We will send renewal reminders 30 days before expiry via email and SMS. You can renew online through our website or mobile app. Grace period of 30 days is provided for renewal.

    What if I'm not satisfied with the policy? +

    We offer a 30-day free look period. If you're not satisfied, you can cancel the policy and get a full refund of premium (minus proportionate risk premium for days covered and stamp duty).

    Is COVID-19 treatment covered? +

    Yes, COVID-19 treatment including hospitalization, ICU charges, and post-COVID complications are covered under this policy after the initial 30-day waiting period.
