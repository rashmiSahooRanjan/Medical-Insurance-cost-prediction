    ❌ What's Not Covered

    • Cosmetic or Plastic Surgery
      Procedures done purely for aesthetic purposes unless medically necessary due to accident, burn, or disease requiring reconstruction.
    • Self-Inflicted Injuries
      Injuries or illnesses resulting from attempted suicide, self-harm, or intentional exposure to danger or hazardous activities.
    • War & Nuclear Risks
      Injuries arising from war, invasion, foreign enemy actions, terrorism, nuclear radiation, or weapons of mass destruction.
    • Alcohol & Drug Abuse
      Treatments related to chronic alcoholism, drug addiction, substance abuse, or complications arising from such conditions.
    • Experimental & Unproven Treatments
      Treatments that are experimental, investigational, or not recognized by medical councils. Stem cell therapy except approved uses.

    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Who should buy the Premium Elite Plan? +

    This plan is ideal for individuals and families who want maximum protection without compromise. Perfect for frequent travelers needing worldwide coverage, those with pre-existing conditions requiring immediate coverage, families planning for children needing enhanced maternity benefits, high-income professionals seeking comprehensive protection, and anyone who values premium healthcare services and VIP treatment.

    What makes this plan different from Standard Plan? +

    Premium Plan offers ₹10 lakhs coverage (vs ₹5 lakhs), worldwide emergency coverage, zero waiting period for pre-existing diseases (vs 1 year), enhanced maternity coverage (₹75,000 vs ₹50,000), 4 health check-ups (vs 2), 200% sum insured restoration (vs 100%), AYUSH treatment up to ₹50,000 (vs ₹25,000), personal accident cover of ₹5 lakhs, mental health coverage, and priority VIP services at hospitals.

    How does worldwide emergency coverage work? +

    If you face a medical emergency while traveling abroad, your treatment expenses are covered up to policy limits. Coverage includes emergency hospitalization, surgery, ICU, ambulance, medical evacuation to nearest facility, and repatriation back to India if required. You need to inform us within 24 hours of emergency. Coverage applies to trips up to 90 days duration per travel. Planned treatments abroad are not covered.

    Are all pre-existing diseases covered immediately? +

    Yes, this is the only plan with zero waiting period for pre-existing diseases. Conditions like diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, heart disease, asthma, kidney disease, and others are covered from day one. However, you must declare all pre-existing conditions at the time of purchase. Undisclosed conditions may lead to claim rejection. Initial 30-day waiting period applies for all illnesses except accidents.

    What is 200% Sum Insured Restoration? +

    If your entire ₹10 lakhs sum insured is exhausted during a claim, we automatically restore 200% of the sum insured (₹20 lakhs additional) for a different illness in the same policy year. For example, if ₹10 lakhs is used for heart surgery, you get ₹20 lakhs more for any other unrelated illness like cancer. This provides total protection of ₹30 lakhs in one year at no extra cost.

    Is mental health treatment covered? +

    Yes, this is one of the few plans covering mental health. Psychiatric treatment, counseling, therapy sessions, and hospitalization for mental health conditions are covered in IRDA-approved psychiatric facilities up to ₹1 lakh per year. Conditions like depression, anxiety disorders, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, and PTSD are covered. Waiting period of 1 year applies unless due to accident.

    Can I get cashless treatment at any hospital? +

    Cashless treatment is available at 8000+ network hospitals across India. Premium plan members get VIP cashless facility with priority pre-authorization (within 2 hours vs 6-8 hours for other plans), dedicated relationship manager, priority admission, and expedited discharge. For non-network hospitals, you can pay and claim reimbursement. List of network hospitals available on our website and app.

    What is included in Personal Accident Cover? +

    Complimentary personal accident insurance of ₹5 lakhs is included. This pays out in case of accidental death or permanent total disability (loss of two limbs, two eyes, or one limb and one eye). Partial permanent disability is covered proportionately. Temporary total disability provides weekly compensation. This is over and above your health coverage and requires no additional premium.
