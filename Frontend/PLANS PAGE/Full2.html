    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who can buy this Standard Plan? +

    Anyone between 18 to 65 years can purchase this plan. You can cover yourself, spouse, dependent children (3 months to 25 years), and parents. Family floater option is available for up to 6 members. The plan is particularly suitable for families planning for children due to maternity coverage.

    What is included in maternity coverage? +

    Maternity coverage includes normal delivery expenses up to ₹35,000 and C-section up to ₹50,000 per policy period. Newborn baby is automatically covered from day one up to 90 days. Pre and post-natal expenses, delivery room charges, and complications are covered. Maternity waiting period is 9 months.

    What are the waiting periods? +

    Initial waiting period: 30 days for all illnesses (accidents covered from day 1). Pre-existing diseases: 1 year. Specific diseases (hernia, cataract, joint replacement, etc.): 1 year. Maternity: 9 months. No waiting period for accidental hospitalization.

    Can I upgrade from Basic to Standard Plan? +

    Yes, you can upgrade at the time of renewal. Waiting periods already served in Basic Plan will be carried forward. You'll need to pay the difference in premium. Upgrade is subject to underwriting and may require medical examination depending on age.

    How does No Claim Bonus work? +

    For every claim-free year, you earn 10% increase in sum insured without additional premium. Maximum NCB is 50% (after 5 claim-free years). So your ₹5 lakh coverage can increase to ₹7.5 lakhs. NCB is retained even if you switch insurers or change plan variant.

    What is Automatic Restoration benefit? +

    If your sum insured is exhausted during treatment, we automatically restore 100% of the sum insured for a different illness in the same policy year. For example, if ₹5 lakhs is used for heart surgery, you get another ₹5 lakhs for any other unrelated illness. This is provided at no extra cost.

    Are pre and post-hospitalization expenses covered? +

    Yes, medical expenses incurred 60 days before hospitalization (diagnostic tests, doctor consultations) and 90 days after discharge (follow-up consultations, medicines, physiotherapy) are covered. These expenses are part of your sum insured, not over and above.

    Is COVID-19 and related treatments covered? +

    Yes, COVID-19 treatment including hospitalization, ICU charges, oxygen therapy, and post-COVID complications are fully covered. Home care treatment for COVID-19 is also covered. Waiting period is 30 days from policy start date. Vaccination costs are not covered.
